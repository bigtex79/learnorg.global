ILO Learning: courses for whole companies, on learnorg.global

A learning area on ILO's own website. A company buys a course for its team, each person signs in and learns at their own pace, and Bjarne and Samer meet the team live. The company's leader can see how everyone is getting on.

Each person learns at their own pace

Everyone in the team gets their own sign-in. Lessons are short, so they fit into a working week.

  • Short videos of Bjarne and Samer, 5 to 10 minutes each.
  • "Try it this week": a small trial at work after each lesson (plan, do, study, adjust). Learning by doing, the way ILO already teaches.
  • Ticks show what's done, and the course remembers where each person stopped.
  • Works on a phone, tablet or computer.

How we would build it

ILO's own platform, not someone else's

We build the learning area into learnorg.global. We've already built a private course area with sign-in for another client, so this is proven work, not an experiment.

ILO owns it

The courses, the videos and the list of learners belong to ILO. Nothing is locked inside an outside platform.

No monthly platform fee

Ready-made course websites charge every month and often take a share of each sale. Here ILO pays for the build, then only small running costs.

Secure sign-in

Each person has their own sign-in, and course pages stay private. Only people from paying companies can see them.

English and Norwegian

Each course can have both languages, like the rest of the new ILO website.

Simple for ILO

ILO adds a company and its team, and the platform sends the invitations. Invoicing stays as it is today.

Grows step by step

Start with one course. Add quizzes, more courses or card payments later, only when they're needed.

Suggested plan

Start with one course and one company

Step 1

Choose and film

Pick the first course (we suggest Learning Leadership) and film short videos. A phone and a good microphone are enough.

Step 2

Build the course area

We build the course page, lessons, team sign-in and company overview on learnorg.global.

Step 3

Pilot with one client

Run it with one company team, such as an existing client. Gather their feedback, and a few words from them for the website.

Step 4

Offer it to more companies

Improve what the pilot showed, then add the next course: Obeya, or a course for each book.

To get started

What we need from ILO

  • Which course to start with, and which language first.
  • The lesson plan: the topics for each module (the Trippel L material is a good base).
  • Filmed videos, or a day to film them together.
  • Worksheets and templates you already use.
  • One company that would like to be the first team.
