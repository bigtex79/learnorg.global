Books

Books by Bjarne Berg Wig

Norwegian · Gyldendal

Lærende organisasjoner

The book gives leaders, union representatives and other change leaders the knowledge needed to move from an outdated system of goal management, bureaucracy and hierarchy – to a society that removes waste of all forms and frees the vast and often hidden resources in all people.

It describes the necessary shift from goal and result management to real learning organizations and learning systems, in three parts: Past asks why we ended up in this disorder and how we get out of it; Present is about going from “introducing Lean” and continuous improvement to becoming a true learning organization; Future asks how we develop self-organizing and open, participatory organizations.

A red thread throughout the book is systems thinking – awareness of how systems work and how we think about them. To transform organizations, we must change how we think. The book is for people who want to become better thinkers.

Norwegian · Gyldendal

LEAN – Ledelse for lærende organisasjoner

Lean is about creating products and services with the most value and least possible loss of resources, by focusing on flow, transparent processes and continuous improvement.

This book and its digital resources are designed for a breakthrough for Lean: a turning point where continuous improvement and learning in the workplace become commonplace, and all managers and employees in municipalities and businesses are active in improvement and renewal. It is about moving from work as effective execution to seeing work as a learning process.

Part I explains how to develop a breakthrough strategy. Part II covers the methods and tools for introducing and developing Lean in a municipal or private enterprise. Part III, new in this edition, covers real learning organizations: learning leadership and the use of guidance, coaching and problem-solving dialogue – what remains after the term Lean goes into history.

English · Handbook for practitioners

Quality Improvement as a Craft

Quality Improvement as a Craft (Hydro Media, 1996) was the main training material for continuous improvement teams in Norway for many years. It is still very valid and gives an overview of many of the methods and tools we know today under the name Lean. The book is still used in teaching at academic courses.
