On-site Training

Bring an expert to your company

Book an expert to come to your company for hands-on training.

Off-site Training

A change of scenery opens the mind

Take your team off-site to ILO's Albergo del Vino, surrounded by the vineyards of Piemonte, Italy.

Here your team can fully focus on “learning how to learn together”. Programs are tailored to the needs of your organization and cover all key concepts to help participants become systems thinkers. The off-site program is excellent for team building too.

Before your visit we discuss the goals and needs of your team, and create a custom program so your team receives exactly what it needs.

Gemba Learning Trips

Go and see

Crucial to learning is to go and see, communicate and learn from practitioners.

We provide training and learning in partnership with leading companies and municipalities in Norway, Italy and the UK in the field of Lean and learning culture. We organize visits to relevant organizations so participants can see how improvement and change are made, and discuss case studies with the transformation leaders behind them. Combined training and gemba walks provide the ultimate learning experience.
