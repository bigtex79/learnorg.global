Obeya – faster learning together

Obeya is a visual room for collaboration between the different functions of an organisation. Obeya is an architecture for radically better collaboration across boundaries.

By visualizing (seeing together), reflecting and trying out solutions, we learn faster and waste less time on papers sent back and forth. Obeya is also the room for developing a better understanding of systems: better systems thinking.

At our seminars we learn about Obeya and share experience between organisations. We are only at the start of this next Lean journey, which will give us dramatically better learning.
