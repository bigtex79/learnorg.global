Learning Culture Transformation

From seeing work as execution to seeing work as continuous learning and improvement.

Learning Culture Transformation (LCT) is the shift from seeing work as execution to seeing work as continuous learning and improvement – seeing the organization as a complex adaptive system that adapts to change through learning.

Learning culture

LCT is a breakaway from Taylor's "scientific management" model of centralized command-and-control organizations: from command and control to challenging and developing learners. LCT is about deep respect for people and building on people's innate capacity to learn. Dr W. Edwards Deming (1900–1993) put it this way in The New Economics:

"One is born with a natural inclination to learn. Learning is a source of innovation. One inherits a right to enjoy his work. Good management helps us to nurture these positive innate attributes of people."

What is learning?

Learning is the process of developing and improving our mental models – how we see and understand – so we navigate the real world better. It requires a good understanding of the thinking process and of how we acquire knowledge through seeing, dialogue and practical trials. Two insights are key:

  • People are born with learning capacity. Present management practice has reduced these skills, so they have to be nurtured.
  • We all have biases in how we see and interpret the real world (mental models). We need to understand these biases and use that understanding to become better thinkers.

Complex adaptive systems

A complex adaptive system (CAS) is able to adapt to change for better interaction with the real world. In an environment of rapid technological, social, geographical and environmental change, the capacity for real learning is crucial for survival. Nature itself is the best teacher of complex adaptive systems.

What is a learning organization?

An organization that acquires, creates, interprets, transmits, shares and retains knowledge, and consciously adapts its behaviour to reflect new knowledge and insights.
