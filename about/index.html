Who we are

Making systems thinking affordable and accessible

The Institute for Learning Organizations (ILO) was created to make the knowledge of systems thinking affordable and accessible. It evolved from the Total Quality Management Center Norway. ILO has two resident experts, Bjarne Berg Wig and Samer Hamadeh. Together they offer over 30 years of experience in continuous improvement and developing real learning organisations.

Their focus is to help employees and leaders become systems thinkers, and to practically apply this thinking within their organisations, through a combination of mentorship, on-site training and e-learning.

Author · Sensei

Bjarne Berg Wig

Author and sensei Bjarne Berg Wig has more than 30 years of experience in quality management and Lean. He was Quality and Innovation Manager at Hydro Agri Europe (now Yara).

For the last 20 years, Bjarne has helped many Norwegian and international private and public companies transform into learning organisations. He has developed academic programs with Bergen University College in Quality Management and Lean leadership. Several hundred leaders have gone through his practical training, with high impact on many companies and municipalities in Norway.

He has written 7 professional books, which are used both in industry and in academic programs. Bjarne is a founder of LOS Norge, the Society for Learning Organizations in Norway, and is regarded as one of the "gurus" of learning organisations in Europe.

Resident expert

Samer Hamadeh

Samer Hamadeh is a specialist in learning culture transformation: the shift from seeing work as execution to seeing work as continuous learning and improvement.

Samer works alongside Bjarne on ILO's mentorship, training and seminars, helping organisations build a culture where everyone learns and improves every day. He has spoken on learning culture transformation at the Manufacturing and Supply Chain Conference in Dublin, and co-hosts ILO's webinars on how to frame the transformation to a learning culture.
