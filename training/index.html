Off-site Training

A change of scenery helps open the mind

Take your team off-site to ILO's Albergo del Vino, surrounded by the vineyards of Piemonte, Italy.

Here your team can fully focus on "learning how to learn together". Training programs are tailored to the needs of your organisation and cover all key concepts to help participants become systems thinkers. This off-site program is excellent for team building too.

In advance of your visit we will discuss the goals and needs of your team. From this we will create a custom program so that your team receives exactly what it needs.

Gemba Learning Trips

Learn from the people who make the change

Crucial to learning is to go and see, communicate and learn from practitioners.

We provide training and learning in partnership with leading companies and municipalities in Norway, Italy and the UK in the field of Lean and learning culture. We organize visits to relevant organisations so participants get a chance to see how improvement and change are made, and discuss specific case studies with the transformation leaders behind them. This combined training and gemba walks provide the ultimate learning experience.
