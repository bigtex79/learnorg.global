Trippel L – Learning Leadership

A course run jointly by the Institute for Learning Organizations and Flowit.

Book a free consultation

The course ran from December 2023 to June 2024. Get in touch to hear when it runs next.

More than ever, we need to improve the quality of our leadership. Learning leadership sets off a chain reaction of improvement and learning. Every organisation is a "complex adaptive system" where faster learning together is the key to success. It starts with how we think: developing mental models that are closer to how reality really is. This course is about understanding systems, and understanding and improving our thinking.

The programme is a collaboration between the Institute for Learning Organizations and Flowit. Its content was developed partly through a "PhD" programme Bjarne Berg Wig completed with the Cabrera Research Lab in Boston, and partly through two R&D programmes supported by the Research Council of Norway. The first programme (2020–2022) produced a method for identifying strong adaptive learning cultures (the LOS mirror). The second, ALIOS (accelerated learning in organisations and society), is developing methods for greater thinking maturity, systems thinking and systems leadership, drawing on the world's leading research in the field. Guest lecturers come from the ALIOS R&D team: Sidsel W. Storaas, Johannes Borge, Kari Heggholmen, Einar Jørgensen and John Richard Hansen.

The course takes you a significant step further: personally, because you think better and more consciously, and for your organisation, because your leadership has far greater impact. As on earlier Lean courses, we link learning to the real challenges you face, so the course works twice over. All participants have several years of Lean and continuous improvement in their own organisations. The Trippel L programme aims to release potential in you and your colleagues that you hardly thought possible.

A major benefit for you will be studying alongside some of Norway's best in the field.
