What is a sensei?

Look beyond the borders of your first understanding

A sensei encourages you to look beyond the borders of your first understanding of the current situation, toward a deeper meaning.

Whether you are advanced in systems thinking or just starting to use the method, we recommend you enlist the expertise of our sensei mentor to help you learn and grow through your journey.
