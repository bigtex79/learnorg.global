Sensei

Look beyond the borders of your first understanding

Sensei is encouragement to look beyond the borders of your first understanding of the current situation, toward a deeper meaning.

Whether you are advanced in systems thinking or just now starting to use the method, we recommend you enlist the expertise of our sensei mentor to help you with learning and growing through your journey.

Let us know how we can help you
How we work

From first conversation to lasting change

  1. 1Talk

    A free consultation: tell us where you are today and what you want to change.

  2. 2Plan

    Together we agree the goals, the people involved and how we will learn.

  3. 3Mentor

    Regular sensei sessions: challenge, dialogue and practice in your own workplace.

  4. 4Review

    We study the results together and decide the next step.
