Training

Learning how to learn together

Take your team off-site to ILO's Albergo del Vino, surrounded by the vineyards of Piemonte, Italy.

Here your team can fully focus on "learning how to learn together". Training programs are tailored to the needs of your organization and cover all key concepts to help participants become systems thinkers. This off-site program is excellent for team building too.

In advance of your visit we will discuss the goals and needs of your team. From this we will create a custom program so that your team receives exactly what it needs.
